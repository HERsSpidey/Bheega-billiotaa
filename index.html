💌 A letter for a low day

Open whenever you need a gentle reminder.

Hey, princess. ❤️

Please don't feel you have to hide your tears or handle everything by yourself. If you need to cry, it's okay to let your feelings out. I wish I could make every worry smaller and every difficult day easier.

I can't promise everything will become perfect overnight, but I truly hope things get better. You don't have to solve everything all at once, and you don't have to pretend to be okay for anyone.

If you want to talk, I'll listen. If you want a silly distraction, I have some truly terrible jokes ready. If you need space, that's okay too. You deserve patience and care, especially on the hard days.

Take one little step at a time, princess. This moment isn't your whole story. I hope tomorrow feels a little lighter. You don't have to face hard things all by yourself. 🌷

With care,
Your friendly neighbourhood Spidey 🕷️💗
